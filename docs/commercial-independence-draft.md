# AdvertEYES independence, donation and transparency policy

Draft product requirements and counsel-review brief — not an executed contract, legal opinion, completed compliance review or authorization to publish correspondence or personal data.

## Mission and non-negotiable separation

Make the project maximally honest about its own incentives, evidence, uncertainty, payments, mistakes and corrections, even when transparency costs revenue. No sponsorships. A company cannot buy selection, evaluator appointments, access to testers, test methods, ratings, outcomes, favorable coverage, suppression or editorial priority. Product donations buy zero influence. Facts about actual products tested take precedence over commercial relationships.

A product donor may have a financial interest in a reviewed category. That does not make the donor eligible to buy advertising. Nonparticipants are absent from the giveaway-contributor list, without a shaming label, threat, lower rating or suggestion that refusal proves poor quality. Donor recognition is factual source disclosure, not a purchased editorial platform. If only participating products are tested, claims must say best among the tested cohort and disclose the selection process; they cannot imply best across the market.

## Draft advertiser eligibility rule for counsel to finalize

Do not accept advertising, directly or indirectly, from a person or entity with a material financial interest in influencing the final review outcome for the relevant category or review. Examine reviewed-product owners and sellers, relevant competitors, controlling and beneficial owners, parents/subsidiaries/affiliates, agents, intermediaries and the actual funding/beneficiary chain. An agency purchase cannot conceal an ineligible principal. This is an objective financial-interest rule, not a test of whether a buyer admits wanting influence.

The contemplated agreement should require accurate ownership, funding and conflict disclosures; timely updates when circumstances change; proportionate audit rights; and suspension where material information is concealed or eligibility is unresolved. Exact scope, definitions, procedures and remedies require counsel. No automatic legal classification or unapproved broad remedies are asserted here.

### Evidence-backed decision record

For every proposed ad purchase, maintain: proposed buyer, represented principal, actual payer, ultimate beneficiary, ownership/control evidence with source/date, relevant products/categories, competitive interests, disclosed relationships, missing evidence, reviewer, decision, reasons, scope and review expiry. A checkbox or self-attestation alone never proves independence. Missing or contradictory evidence means **pending review**, not eligible. No ads may run while pending. Any approval must cite the evidence and accountable reviewer.

Keep advertising sales, donor intake and product scoring operationally separate. There is no ad SDK or sales flow in the current game.

## Donor-funded products: recipient owes nothing

Binding product requirement: donors bear the complete prize-related financial burden, including product cost, shipping, sales/use taxes, fees and applicable recipient income-tax burden. Any necessary assistance must itself be grossed up so tax on the assistance does not leave the recipient owing money. The platform does not silently pay a donor’s shortfall. If a reviewed mechanism cannot satisfy the zero-recipient-cost requirement, do not admit or promise the giveaway.

A literal $0 checkout is necessary but not sufficient to establish zero tax burden. Legal reporting obligations may remain; do not call prizes legally tax-free. Counsel and a qualified tax professional must design eligibility, reporting, valuation, donor funding, gross-up and reconciliation before any real giveaway. No real checkout, tax filing or money movement is implemented.

Products may be allocated randomly under a future properly reviewed giveaway structure. A possible later donor participation/administration fee is an unresolved commercial design, not an implemented price or agreement. It cannot buy a rating or favorable selection, and there is no guaranteed sales lift.

## Required reviews versus unconditional prizes

A random unconditional prize and goods supplied in exchange for a required review are different structures. The latter is proposed review compensation/contracted activity, not automatically an independent-contractor relationship just because a 1099 might be used. Final classification depends on the actual relationship and applicable rules; it requires professional review.

Any review arrangement must request an honest positive **or negative** review and disclose product compensation. No favorable-review condition, rating-based penalty, suppression or undisclosed endorsement. Proposed participant accountability (user suggested a public noncompliance list; neutral overdue/disputed/completed statuses were proposed instead) remains unresolved. No public handles or alleged noncompliance are published. Any eventual obligation needs advance disclosure, reminders, a reasonable response/dispute process and an approved privacy-preserving design.

The FTC explains that free products/material connections can require clear disclosure; editorial independence alone does not eliminate that issue. See [FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) and [FTC guidance on soliciting and paying for reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/soliciting-paying-online-reviews-guide-marketers). Worker classification is not established by a label; see [IRS worker-classification guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-self-employed-or-employee). Application to the proposed program remains for professional review.

## Public evidence ledger: architecture requirement, not implemented

Future public append-only records should explain project money in/out, donation and advertiser decisions, relevant reviewed correspondence, selection methods, test evidence, score methods, conflicts, uncertainties and corrections. Link claims to reviewable evidence. Preserve correction history and visible redaction notices; do not silently rewrite inconvenient entries. A hash chain cannot itself prove that evidence is complete or truthful.

Separate private source vault from public redacted projections. Personal information, credentials, financial account details and legally protected material stay private. Public publication needs item-specific review and authorization; broad transparency aspirations do not authorize exposing a mailbox, full financial records or another person’s private correspondence. Collect only necessary information, resolve publication rights and retention, and record why information is withheld. No real emails, financial data, donor names or participant records have been added or published by this implementation.

### Meaning of timely completion — unresolved operational details

A review deadline should be explicit, product-specific and agreed before acceptance, measured from confirmed delivery. A 30-day consumable review window was proposed, not approved as a universal rule. Durability tests may need longer periods. Delivery delays, unusable/defective samples and justified extensions require a documented process. Compensation is for completing an honest review, never its sentiment. No deadline, collection process or participant penalty has been activated.

## Recipient review pages — future scope

Requested: each recipient can publish an eight-minute review video on a personal review page. Optional creator assistance may help with structure, jokes or fill-in prompts grounded in the recipient's actual experience and sentiment. Fully independent videos must be equally supported. Recipients approve every assisted line; no invented experiences, favorable-verdict requirements or extra friction for negative reviews. Disclose product compensation and substantive platform scripting. Preserve versioned raw, assisted and final drafts plus approval provenance privately; publish only explicitly approved final material.

The user wants project material on GitHub. Prepare public-ready source, specifications, templates and approved redacted evidence separately from private participant drafts/raw videos and protected records. Private storage may hold originals with approved hash references. Repository creation/access and a signed publication route must be verified before any push; no destination or credentials from other projects may be repurposed. This local implementation has not published anything.
